How to Write Winning Proposals

Stand out from the competition with these tips

Do's

  • Personalize each proposal
  • Address the client by name
  • Show understanding of project
  • Share relevant experience
  • Ask thoughtful questions
  • Be clear about timeline
  • Proofread before sending

Don'ts

  • Send generic templates
  • Undersell your rates
  • Make unrealistic promises
  • Ignore project requirements
  • Use unprofessional language
  • Forget to attach samples
  • Bid on every project

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about finding work

How do I get my first project?

Start by completing your profile fully, then bid on smaller projects to build your reputation. Consider offering a competitive rate initially to attract first clients.

What should I charge?

Research market rates for your skill level. Start competitive but don't undervalue yourself. You can increase rates as you gain reviews.

How many proposals should I send?

Quality over quantity. Send 3-5 well-researched proposals daily rather than 20 generic ones. Focus on projects that match your skills.

How long until I get hired?

It varies, but with a strong profile and good proposals, many freelancers get their first project within 1-2 weeks.

Can I work with multiple clients?

Yes! You can work with multiple clients simultaneously. Just ensure you can manage your time and meet all deadlines.

How do I handle difficult clients?

Communicate clearly, set expectations upfront, and use our dispute resolution if needed. Most issues can be resolved with good communication.
